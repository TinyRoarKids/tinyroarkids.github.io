Meet Leo and his friends

Nine friends, nine different talents, one big group that always sticks together.

T-rex

Leo

The biggest of the group and the softest heart. He is strong, curious and a little afraid of the dark.

Velociraptor

Max

The fastest dino around. Always in a hurry, slowly learning that some things are worth waiting for.

Triceratops

Emma

Brave and protective, a bit stubborn, always the first to stand up for a friend.

Stegosaurus

Ben

Slow, calm and funny. He reminds everyone to take a deep breath.

Brachiosaurus

Ava

Her long neck lets her see the whole world. Shy, but full of good ideas.

Ankylosaurus

Sam

Small and sturdy, he loves building anything out of blocks.

Pteranodon

Zoe

She flies high and brings news from far away. (Fun fact: pteranodons aren't dinosaurs, but she's still part of the family!)

Elasmosaurus

Ollie

The chatty friend from the lagoon who tells stories from under the water.

Parasaurolophus

Grandma Rose

Kind and wise, she plays music with her crest and always knows what to say.
