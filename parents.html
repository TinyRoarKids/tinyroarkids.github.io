For parents and teachers

We make the kind of videos we'd happily watch next to our own children.

Our promises

  • No scary or violent scenes. No villains.
  • No products, toys or brands to sell.
  • Calm pacing, clear voices, big readable subtitles.
  • Every story has something gentle to learn.
  • We never ask children to comment, subscribe or share.
  • Our videos are set as "made for kids" on YouTube.

How we make our stories

Every episode starts with an idea and a lesson chosen by our team. We use AI tools to help draw and animate our world, and every story is written, checked and approved by people before it is published.

Use in classrooms

Teachers are welcome to use our videos in class. Write to us for themed playlists.
